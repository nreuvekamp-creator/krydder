About & licenses

The AI voice runs entirely in your browser with these open-source parts (license texts in vendor/piper/LICENSES, exact versions in VERSIONS.txt):

  • Piper text-to-speech, MIT (license)
  • Voice model "nvcc" (no_NO-nvcc-medium, speaker KON), trained on Språkbanken recordings, CC0 (dataset, model card)
  • onnxruntime-web 1.31.1, MIT (license)
  • piper-phonemize WebAssembly build (@diffusionstudio/piper-wasm 1.0.0), MIT (license)
  • espeak-ng, GPL-3.0 (license): version 1.52-dev, the rhasspy fork at commit 8593723 (source code). Only the Norwegian data files are included.

The fonts are self-hosted, so they also work offline:

  • Fraunces, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (license)
  • Schibsted Grotesk, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (license)
